Design a luxurious, high-end pitch demo for **Ripple**, a mobile app that turns public disclosures by the people who move markets into one calm, readable feed. The audience is investors watching a pitch. It should feel like a premium fintech or private-bank product launch: quiet, confident, editorial. It should not look like a crypto hype deck.

## Deliverable
A 16:9 pitch deck of 10 slides, built as an animated click-through prototype so it can be presented live and screen-recorded as a demo video. Each slide shows a phone mockup of a real app screen next to a short, editorial headline. Transitions are slow and cinematic: phones rise and fade in, text eases up, and screens crossfade within a flow. Avoid bouncy or playful motion.

## Assets I'm attaching
- `ripple-original-1.png`: the app logo, a rounded "R" built from concentric layers of indigo, royal blue and sky blue.
- 20 real app screenshots at 1170×2532, dark mode, with filenames like `06-feed-personal.png`. Use them as the phone screens exactly as they are. Do not redraw, re-type or invent app UI or data.

## Brand system
- **Colours** (from the logo):
  - Ink page `#07060F`
  - Deep core `#150438`
  - Indigo `#1F0766`
  - Royal `#1B42A9`
  - Sky accent `#21A1E4`
  - Text `#F4F3FB`
  - Muted text `#B2ADCE`
  - Faint text `#787298`
  - App surfaces `#0B0918` / `#141127`
- **Background:** a near-black ink page with a large, soft indigo glow centred behind the phone, a smaller royal-blue glow offset low and to the right, a faint sky-blue glow above, and a dark vignette at the edges.
- **Signature motif:** very fine concentric rings (a ripple) radiating from behind the phone. They are thin sky-blue hairlines at about 10–15% opacity, spaced geometrically, and fading out with distance. In motion they expand slowly outward. This motif is the brand: keep it subtle and keep it on every slide.
- **Typography:**
  - Headlines: a high-contrast Didone serif (Bodoni Moda or similar), regular weight, sentence case, set large with generous leading.
  - Body: a light humanist sans (Inter Light or similar) in muted lavender-grey.
  - Section labels: small caps in a semibold sans, letterspaced wide, in sky blue, followed by a short sky-blue rule, e.g. "01 — THE FEED".
- **Device:** a minimal dark phone frame with a hairline lavender metallic rim, rounded corners and a soft, deep drop shadow. On two-phone slides, the back phone is offset up and to the right and dimmed about 25%.
- **Layout:** text block on the left (about the left 40%), phone(s) centred on the right. Lots of negative space. A small logo plus the word "Ripple" sits in the bottom-left footer.

## Rules
- Only use the real screenshots and the exact copy above. Do not invent metrics, user numbers, returns, testimonials, press logos or performance claims.
- Do not imply investment advice or guaranteed returns. The product presents public records with market context.
- Keep contrast accessible: body text at least 4.5:1 against the background.
- No stock photography, no emoji, no gradients on text, no neon glow on the phone frames.
- Mood references: the restraint of an Apple keynote, the typography of a luxury-fashion editorial, the palette of deep midnight blue.
